## 🚀 About Me

I am a passionate **Tech Manager, Data Architect, and DevOps Consultant** with over 12 years of experience. I specialize in auditing, optimizing, and securing critical database infrastructures, as well as orchestrating modern containerized environments.

* 🛠️ **Data Stack:** Expert tuning & consulting for **MySQL**, **PostgreSQL**, and **MongoDB**.
* 🌐 **Infrastructure & DevOps:** Automation, high availability, and cloud architectures utilizing **Docker** & **Kubernetes**.
* 🌟 **Open Source:** Creator and main maintainer of [**MySQLTuner**](https://github.com/jmrenouard/mysqlTuner), a widely used database diagnostic tool.
